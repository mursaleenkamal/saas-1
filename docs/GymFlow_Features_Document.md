## 2. Core Modules & Feature Breakdown

### 📊 Module 1: Real-Time Executive Dashboard
* **Today's Collections:** Instant total of all cash, Raast, and digital payments collected today.
* **Active vs. Expired Member Split:** Live count of members eligible to work out vs. those with expired packages.
* **Overdue Dues Sentinel:** Highlights exactly how much money is pending across all overdue members.
* **Revenue Trends:** Visual breakdown of monthly memberships vs. personal training vs. supplement sales.

### 💬 Module 2: WhatsApp Automated Dues Recovery Engine
* **1-Tap Direct WhatsApp Web / Cloud API:** Send pre-formatted, polite Urdu & English reminders.
* **Dynamic Smart Link:** Includes member's exact name, package name, outstanding balance, and gym Raast QR code.
* **Automated Expiry Triggers:** 3-day prior warning, expiry day notification, and overdue grace period alerts.

### ⚡ Module 3: High-Speed Front-Desk Attendance
* **1-Second Kiosk Mode:** Type a 4-digit Member ID (e.g., `GF-0142`) or scan a digital QR card.
* **Visual Instant Feedback:**
  * 🟢 **GREEN:** Active Membership (Welcome screen with remaining days).
  * 🔴 **RED:** Expired Membership (Immediate prompt to collect renewal fee).
  * 🟡 **AMBER:** Expiring in < 3 days.
* **Shift Tracking:** Morning and Evening session management with duplicate check-in prevention.

### 💳 Module 4: Multi-Payment & Raast EMVCo QR Integration
* **Raast Instant Counter QR:** Generates dynamic or static Raast QR codes for fee settlement with zero gateway fees.
* **Wallets & Cash:** Supports EasyPaisa, JazzCash, Bank Transfer, and physical Cash Ledgers.
* **Split Fee Accounting:** Automatically separates one-time Admission Fees from recurring monthly membership plans.
* **Instant Digital Receipts:** Members receive digital SMS / WhatsApp payment confirmation slips.

### 👥 Module 5: Comprehensive Member Digital Records
* **Digital Profile:** Photo, CNIC, Phone number (auto-normalized to `+92`), Emergency Contact, Blood Group, and Medical notes.
* **Membership Plan Management:** 1-Month, 3-Month, 6-Month, Annual, and Custom Personal Training (PT) packages.
* **1-Tap Renewal & Freeze:** Easily renew plans or apply medical freeze periods without corrupting billing cycles.

### 🥤 Module 6: Gym Inventory & Supplement POS
* **Counter Sales:** Track retail sales of protein scoops, pre-workout drinks, energy bars, and mineral water.
* **Real-Time Stock Alerts:** Notifies when supplement stock drops below minimum thresholds.
* **Profit Margin Reporting:** Tracks purchase cost vs. selling price for accurate retail profit calculation.

### 📁 Module 7: 1-Click Excel / Register Bulk Migration
* **Zero Downtime Switch:** GymFlow team migrates existing paper register or Excel sheets in under 2 minutes.
* **Automatic Phone Normalization:** Cleans malformed phone numbers into international WhatsApp-ready formats.
